Gravity Flip

Genre: Platformer + Twist: Gravity Manipulation

Controls:
Arrow Keys / WASD - Move
SPACE - Flip Gravity
R - Restart
Score: 0
Level: 1

Collect all stars to advance!

About the Game

Mechanic: Navigate through platforms by flipping gravity to reach the ceiling or floor. This twist on traditional platformers requires players to think spatially and time their gravity flips carefully.

Objective: Collect all the stars in each level to progress. Avoid falling off the screen!
